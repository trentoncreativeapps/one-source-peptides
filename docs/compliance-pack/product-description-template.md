## BPC-157

**Chemical Profile**

BPC-157 is a synthetic pentadecapeptide composed of 15 amino acids, derived from
a partial sequence of body protection compound (BPC) identified in human gastric
juice. Molecular formula and sequence data are provided on the certificate of
analysis accompanying each lot. Purity is determined by high-performance liquid
chromatography (HPLC) and is reported per-lot at time of shipment.

**In Vitro / Preclinical Research Data**

Published in vitro and animal-model literature has examined BPC-157 in the
context of gastrointestinal tissue models, angiogenesis assays, and
musculoskeletal repair models. Findings in these research contexts have been
associated with observed effects on cell migration and tissue markers under
laboratory conditions. This summary reflects published research literature and
is provided for reference only; it does not constitute a claim about effects in
humans or animals, and no such claim should be inferred.

**Format & Handling**

Supplied as a lyophilized powder. Recommended storage: -20°C for long-term
storage; reconstituted solution should be stored per standard peptide handling
protocols and used within the timeframe indicated on the certificate of
analysis. Handling should be performed only by trained laboratory personnel
using appropriate PPE and equipment.

**Certificate of Analysis**

Each lot ships with a corresponding certificate of analysis (COA) confirming
identity and purity via HPLC and mass spectrometry. COA is available on request
prior to purchase for institutional buyers.

### Banned vocabulary — do not use on any research-side listing
- "Take," "dose," "inject," "administer," "use daily," or any administration
  instruction directed at a person
- "Treats," "cures," "heals," "prevents," "boosts," "improves your," or any
  outcome claim directed at a human or animal condition
- "Safe," "effective," "clinically proven" outside of a citation to specific
  published literature
- Any second-person language implying personal use ("you'll notice," "helps
  you")
- Testimonials, before/after framing, or dosing calculators
