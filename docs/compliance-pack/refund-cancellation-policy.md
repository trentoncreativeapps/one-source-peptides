## Order Cancellation

Orders may be cancelled without charge if the request is made before the order
has shipped. Once an order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled.

## Returns

Due to the nature of laboratory reference materials — including cold-chain
handling, lot-specific certificates of analysis, and the inability to verify
storage conditions after a product leaves our facility — products **cannot be
returned** once delivered, except in the following cases:

- The product received does not match the item ordered (wrong SKU/lot)
- The product arrived with visible damage to packaging affecting product
  integrity
- Independent lab testing (at Buyer's expense) demonstrates the product does
  not meet the purity specification on its certificate of analysis

## Refund Process

Approved refund or replacement requests must be submitted within 7 days of
delivery, with photographic evidence (for damage claims) or third-party lab
results (for purity disputes). Approved refunds are issued to the original
payment method within 10 business days.

## Non-Refundable Circumstances

- Buyer error in product selection or quantity
- Products stored or handled outside recommended conditions after delivery
- Any claim submitted more than 7 days after delivery
- Any product that has had its RUO labeling removed or altered

## Chargebacks

Buyers are asked to contact the Company directly to resolve any order issue
before initiating a chargeback with their payment provider. Fraudulent
chargebacks may result in account suspension and referral to the Company's
high-risk payment processor's dispute resolution process.
